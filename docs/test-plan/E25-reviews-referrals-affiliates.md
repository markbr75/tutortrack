# E25 — Reviews, Referrals & Affiliates: manual tests

**Scope.** Review requests, the public review page, moderation and rating aggregates; testimonials and the Google review prompt; the client referral programme with fraud checks and credit rewards; affiliates with tracking links, the partner portal, commission, clawbacks, statements and bank-file payouts; tutor referral bonuses; the **Review request** built-in workflow (E33; it replaced `ReviewRequestWorkflow`), `ReferralRewardWorkflow` and `AffiliateStatementRunWorkflow`. Spec: [E25](../epics/E25-reviews-referrals-affiliates.md).

**Built differently from the spec (Implementation notes win):**
- The `tt_ref` cookie is set only with marketing cookie consent (E29). Without consent the code is kept for the browser session only.
- A "free lesson" reward is account credit of the programme's reward amount. The referee's discount is an E06 client discount rule, not a promo code.
- Commission is based on the collected payment amount, not on invoice allocations, so prepayments also earn commission.
- Stripe Connect payouts for affiliates are not built. Use the bank file or "Mark paid".
- Portal clients can't leave reviews unprompted. Reviews come from requests or from staff.
- There are no E26 referral, affiliate or review reports.

**Common preconditions.**
- Stack running with Temporal worker (`manage.py temporal_worker --task-queue all`), Celery worker and Mailpit (http://localhost:8025). Data loaded with `make seed`.
- Admin app: http://brightminds.localhost:5173. Portal: http://brightminds.localhost:5174/portal/. Password for every seeded account: `tutortrack` (or `SEED_ADMIN_PASSWORD`).
- Seed: referral programme "Refer a friend" (£20 reward, 10% welcome discount, max 5 rewards per period); three staff-entered reviews of Nia Adeyemi (two approved: Priya P. 5★, James W. 4★; one pending: Sofia M. 5★); affiliate "Hillside Primary PTA" (organisation) with a Main link; default commission rule "Standard partner rate" 10% for 12 months; `affiliate@tutortrack.localhost` linked to Hillside Primary PTA.
- The admin screen is **Reviews & referrals** (`/growth`), with tabs Reviews, Review requests, Referrals, Affiliates, Statements, Tutor referrals and Settings.
- Payments in dev use the fake Stripe provider. To create a "successful payment" quickly, record a manual payment on an issued invoice (Billing → invoice → Record payment), or pay the invoice in the portal with the fake card.
