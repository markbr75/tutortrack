# Data processing agreement (version 2026-10)

Tenants accept this agreement in the app (Privacy centre → Agreement & sub-processors;
owner only, `privacy.dpa.manage`). Each acceptance records the version, the signer's name and
role, the organisation's legal name, the time, IP address and browser
(`privacy.DPAAcceptance`), and publishes `dpa.accepted`. A new version needs a new acceptance.

## Summary of terms
1. **Roles.** The organisation is the controller of its clients', students' and tutors' data;
   TutorTrack is its processor (UK GDPR / EU GDPR Article 28). For our own account data
   (owners' logins, billing) we are a controller.
2. **Instructions.** We process personal data only to provide the service and on documented
   instructions (the product configuration counts as instructions).
3. **Confidentiality.** Our staff are bound by confidentiality and see customer data only
   when the customer grants support access, or to meet a legal obligation.
4. **Security.** The measures in the information security policy, including encryption at
   rest and in transit, field encryption for sensitive data, tenancy isolation enforced by
   the database, audit logging and annual penetration tests.
5. **Sub-processors.** The current list is [subprocessors.md](subprocessors.md). We give
   notice of changes in the app; the customer may object within 30 days.
6. **Data subject rights.** The product provides the tools (DSAR workflow, export, erasure,
   consent records). We help with requests we receive directly.
7. **Breaches.** We notify the customer without undue delay and within 48 hours of becoming
   aware of a personal data breach affecting their data ([breach-notification.md](breach-notification.md)).
8. **Location.** Data stays in the organisation's region (UK, EU, US or Australia); transfers
   to sub-processors outside it use SCCs and the UK addendum.
9. **Deletion.** On account closure, data is exported on request and deleted after the grace
   period (E02 closure process), except where the law requires us to keep it.
10. **Audits.** We provide our SOC 2 report (when available) and answer reasonable security
    questionnaires once a year.
