TutorTrack user guide

How to use TutorTrack day to day: for the office team in the admin app, tutors in the tutor app, families and students in the client portal, and affiliates in the partner portal.

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Setting up the organisation (settings, roles, payments, integrations, the website and data import) is covered in the admin guide. Developers should read the API guide.

Getting started

TutorTrack runs a tutoring business: people, lessons, reports, invoices, payments and tutor pay. Each tutoring business (an organisation) has its own web address, for example brightminds.tutortrack.app, and its own data. Nobody outside the organisation can see it.

The apps and who uses them

Admin app

For the office team: owners, admins, branch managers, coordinators and finance staff. Open the organisation's address. See Office team.

Tutor app

For tutors, on a phone or computer, at /portal/tutor. Installable and works offline. See Tutors.

Client portal

For parents, guardians, adult learners and students, at /portal/. See Clients and students.

Partner portal

For affiliates who refer new families, at /portal/affiliate. See Affiliates.

You don't need to remember which app is yours. If you sign in to the admin app with a tutor, client or student login, TutorTrack sends you to the right place.

Signing in

You join an organisation by invitation. Open the link in the invitation email and select Create your account to accept (or sign in first if you already have a TutorTrack account, then Accept invitation). One login can belong to several organisations, for example a tutor who works for two agencies.

The Sign in page offers these ways in. Your organisation may turn some of them off.

MethodHow
Email and passwordEnter your Email and Password and select Sign in. Tick Keep me signed in on this device on your own device only.
Email linkEnter your email and select Email me a sign-in link. The link works once and expires in 15 minutes.
PasskeySelect Sign in with a passkey and use your fingerprint, face or device PIN. Add passkeys on your account.
Google, Microsoft or AppleSelect Continue with Google, Continue with Microsoft or Continue with Apple. Use the account with the same email address as your TutorTrack login.

Forgot your password? Select Forgot your password?, enter your email and select Send reset link. The link lasts one hour. Choosing a new password signs you out everywhere else.

Two-factor authentication (2FA). If you turned on 2FA, or your organisation requires it for staff, TutorTrack asks for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app after your password. You can enter a recovery code instead, or select Use a passkey instead. To set up 2FA, go to Account › Two-factor authentication, select Set up authenticator app, scan the QR code, enter the code and select Turn on. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe; each works once.

Families and students sign in to the portal with an emailed sign-in link. Children under 13 can have a username and PIN made by their parent (see signing in to the portal).

Staying signed in

Staff are signed out after 8 hours without activity (your organisation can change this), or after 30 days with Keep me signed in on this device. After 5 wrong attempts, sign-in slows down for a while to stop password guessing.

Finding your way in the admin app

  • Sidebar: the menu down the left, grouped by job: People, Scheduling, Lessons and learning, Money, Sales and recruitment, Communication and Insight. On a phone, open it with Open menu at the top left. It only lists pages your role can use, so two colleagues can see different menus.
  • Settings: at the bottom of the sidebar. It opens the settings area, which has its own menu: Organisation, Teaching and money, Communication, Data and website and Trust and safety. The admin guide describes each settings page.
  • Search in the bar along the top (Ctrl+K or ⌘+K): type at least two letters of a name, email or phone number to find clients, contacts, students, tutors, jobs and invoices. Use the arrow keys and Enter to open one.
  • Ask AI (Ctrl+J or ⌘+J): if your organisation uses the AI assistant. See AI copilot and command bar.
  • Notifications bell in the top bar: things that need your attention; a red number shows how many are new. See notifications.
  • Help: search help Articles, read What's new, take guided Tours (for example "Schedule lessons" and "Invoices and payments"), or Chat with support. A dot shows new items. Press Esc to stop a tour.
  • Switch organisation: at the bottom of the sidebar if you belong to more than one organisation. Each organisation opens at its own address.
  • Sign out: open the account menu (your initials at the top right), then select Sign out. Your Account page is in the same menu.

Banners at the top of the page tell you about service problems, your organisation's subscription, or, when you use View as to see the app as another user, who you are viewing as ("You're viewing as …"), with Stop viewing as to return.

Working on your own

TutorTrack works for one tutor as well as for an agency. If you run your tutoring on your own, sign up with Type of business set to Sole trader, and TutorTrack keeps the team tools out of your way:

  • You are the tutor as well as the owner. The setup wizard asks whether you teach (I teach lessons myself, ticked for sole traders). Leave it ticked and you become a tutor on your own login, so you can put yourself on lessons. If you skipped it, go to Tutors and select Add yourself as a tutor. The wizard doesn't ask you to invite tutors.
  • A shorter menu. Sole traders don't see Matching, Payroll, Recruitment or Compliance, which are for running a team of tutors. On Home, the money line shows what is Not yet invoiced instead of the next pay run. When you take on tutors, change Type of business under Settings › Organisation and the team menus come back.
  • The tutor app on your phone. Because you teach, the account menu (your initials at the top right) has Open the tutor app. It opens Today at /portal/tutor, which you can install on your phone. See Tutors: the tutor app.
  • The Solo plan. After the 30-day trial a sole trader moves to the Solo plan, for one tutor and up to 40 active students. See plans in the admin guide.

A week on your own

  1. Add a family: on Home select Add a family (or go to Clients).
  2. Set up their lessons: open the student and select Set up lessons, choose the service, yourself as the Tutor, the day and the time. For a one-off lesson, select Book a lesson on Home. See Jobs.
  3. Teach and mark the lesson: on your phone, open the tutor app; Today lists your lessons and Open and complete takes the register and the report. At a computer, mark lessons from Unconfirmed lessons or the lesson's quick view in the Calendar.
  4. Invoice: each completed lesson waits under Billing › Not yet invoiced until it goes on an invoice, as your invoicing style sets out (see run invoices and chase payment).
  5. Get paid: families pay online if you connected card payments; record bank transfers and cash yourself (see record a payment).

Office team: the admin app

The admin app is where the office runs the business: people, the calendar, lessons, billing, payroll, sales and messages. Open your organisation's address (for example https://brightminds.tutortrack.app, or http://brightminds.localhost:5173 on a development machine) and sign in.

This part follows the sidebar, grouped by job. You only see the items your role allows; the permission each page needs is given in its section.

Home

Home greets you by name with today's date and the number of lessons today. Add a family and Book a lesson are at the top right, if your role can do them. Each part below shows only if your role allows it.

  • Money this month (reporting.finance.view, reporting.payroll.view): revenue this month and how it compares with last month, the amount Outstanding and how much of it is overdue, and the Next pay run (for a sole trader, what is Not yet invoiced instead). Select a heading to open the report.
  • Today's register (scheduling.lesson.view): today's lessons in time order, with the tutor and a mark for each one: a tick for Completed, a red ring for Missed, a struck ring for Cancelled, a red dashed ring for a lesson that is over but Not marked yet, and a pale dashed ring for one Still to come. A red line marks the time now. Marked 3 of 7 counts the lessons that have a result.
  • Needs marking (scheduling.lesson.complete): past lessons nobody has marked, with how many days ago each one was. See unconfirmed lessons.
  • Overdue invoices (billing.invoice.view): the oldest unpaid invoices past their due date, with the amount still owed.

New organisations see a Get started checklist (set up a service, add a student, schedule a lesson, connect Stripe, send your first invoice). Each step ticks itself when you do it anywhere in the app. Owners and admins can select Hide checklist.

Below these, if you can see reports (reporting.dashboard.view), the Dashboard shows the figures you choose, with a default layout for your role:

  • Choose the Period (this month by default) and what to Compare with: Previous period, Same period last year or No comparison.
  • Select Open report on a figure to see the full report.
  • Select Customise to add figures, move them with Earlier and Later, change their size or remove them. Then Save layout. Back to the default restores the standard layout for your role.

People

A client is the billing account: a family, an adult learner or an organisation such as a school. A client has contacts (parents, guardians, finance contacts; one is the bill payer) and students. Tutors deliver the lessons.

Clients, students and tutors

Clients, Students and Tutors are lists with one filter bar above the table: a search box, a status list (Any status) and a sort order (Newest first), then Apply. Client and student lists can also filter on lessons booked (Lessons booked: any, Has a lesson booked, No lesson booked); the client list has Only those who owe money. The buttons at the top right of the page hold the list's actions, such as Add a family.

Balance and lesson figures only show if you also have billing or scheduling access. Export CSV and Export Excel, at the top right, export the list with your current filters. On a phone, swipe the table sideways to see every column.

Add a family. On Clients, select Add a family. Enter the parent or guardian, one or more students (Add another student) and the billing address, then select Create family. See the step-by-step recipe.

Teach too? If you run the business and also teach, select Add yourself as a tutor on Tutors (it needs people.tutor.create). You become an active tutor on your own login, with no invitation, so you can be put on lessons and use the tutor app. The button goes once you are a tutor. See working on your own.

Bulk actions. Tick rows (or Select all shown) to:

  • Send a message by email or text. Choose whether it is a service message or Marketing; marketing only goes to people who agreed to receive it.
  • Invite to the portal (clients).
  • Export selected (CSV) or Export selected (Excel).
  • Merge the two selected when exactly two clients or two students are selected (see Duplicates).

The bar shows progress and lists any records that failed, with the reason (for example "no marketing consent").

The record pages
  • Client: Contacts (with Invite to portal for each contact with an email), Students, billing facts and, with billing access, the client's balances, invoices, payments, packages and payment methods (see billing on the client record).
  • Student: details (year group, school, date of birth, subjects, goals, learning needs), Needs for matching (exam boards and, for staff who may see sensitive details, special educational needs and the family's tutor gender preference when your organisation allows it; Edit to change them), Change status, the student's jobs with Set up lessons, and privacy and safeguarding information.
  • Tutor: details, pay rate, how they teach, Subjects (approve new subjects with Approve), Matching profile (SEN experience, Exam boards and, with people.tutor.view_sensitive, Gender, which is only used for families' gender preferences), pay profile, compliance and background checks, and Change status.

Every record (and every job) has an Activity panel:

  • Notes: Add a note and choose Who can see this: Staff only, Staff and tutors or Shared with the client.
  • Tasks: add a task with a due date; tick it when done. Tasks appear in My tasks.
  • Timeline: notes, tasks, documents, changes, messages, lessons, invoices and payments in date order.
  • Documents: Add document with a category. Tick Ask them to sign it in their portal for click-to-sign. Signature details shows who signed, when and from where, and lets you download the signed copy.
Households

A household is a client with several contacts and students. A parent who belongs to two households (for example after a separation) sees both in the portal and can switch between them.

Map

Map ("Students and tutors on a map") shows roughly where students and tutors are, to help you pair them by distance. It needs people.student.view.

  • Choose what to Show, the Status and Subject, and tick Show tutors' travel radius.
  • Students are circles and tutors are squares. Select a marker and Open record. View as List gives the same people in a table.
  • Positions are approximate (about 100 m) and the map never shows a street address. It is a simple plot, not a street map.
  • If some people can't be placed yet, select Find their map positions and refresh in a minute.

Duplicates and merging

Duplicates ("Possible duplicates") lists pairs of families, contacts or students that look the same, with the reason: same email, same phone number, same name, or same name and date of birth. You need people.merge (owners and admins).

  1. Select Review and merge on a pair, or Merge with another record on a record page.
  2. Choose the record to keep. Keep the other record instead swaps them.
  3. Under Choose the values to keep, pick the value for each field that differs.
  4. Check What moves to the record you keep and any warnings under Before you merge.
  5. Tick I understand that merging can't be undone. and select Merge records.

Lessons, invoices, payments, notes and files move to the record you keep. The other record is archived with a link to the one that was kept. Merge families before merging their contacts or students. Tutors can't be merged.

My tasks

My tasks lists your open tasks from every record, including tasks created by TutorTrack (for example an escalated overdue report). Tick Only overdue tasks to focus on what's late. Tick a task to complete it. Needs crm.task.view.

Scheduling

A job is an ongoing arrangement between a client, one or more students and tutors. A job's lessons usually come from a weekly series. You can also schedule one-off lessons.

Calendar

The Calendar shows lessons and events. It needs scheduling.lesson.view.

  • Views: Day, Week, Month, Agenda, and the resource views Tutors and Rooms (one column per tutor or room). Move with Previous, Today and Next.
  • Filter by Tutor and Status, and Colour by service, tutor, status or location. These sit in the row under the dates.
  • More options opens the less-used tools:
    • Also show times in adds a second time zone, useful when tutors or families are abroad.
    • Print timetable (PDF) prints the current view (up to 31 days) without prices.
    • Save filters saves the current filters; reopen a set from Saved filters.

Each lesson is tinted in its colour and carries the same register mark as the Home page: a tick for completed, a pale dashed ring for still to come, a dashed red ring once it has ended and needs marking, a red ring for missed and a struck-through ring for cancelled. Today's date is in red, and in the day, week and Tutors views a red line marks the time now. The agenda lists each day's lessons in time order.

Schedule a lesson or a series
  1. Select New lesson, or click an empty time (in the Tutors view this fills in the tutor).
  2. Choose the Service, the Tutor (or No tutor yet) and the students (Add another student for a group).
  3. Enter the Date, start time (From) and Minutes.
  4. For a regular lesson, tick Repeat weekly, choose the days, and when it Ends: after a number of lessons or on a date.
  5. Select Schedule lesson or Schedule series.

A series skips closures and dates when the tutor is busy, and tells you how many dates it skipped. Open-ended series keep extending ahead automatically. Lessons for a job are normally set up from the student page with Set up lessons (see Jobs).

Conflicts

TutorTrack checks every new or moved lesson.

  • Blocking conflicts show "Scheduling conflict": the tutor is already teaching, at an event or on approved time off, or the job's hours cap would be passed. People with scheduling.override_conflicts can select Schedule anyway. A double-booked room and a time held for an online booking can never be overridden.
  • Warnings don't block: a student clash, outside the tutor's availability, not enough travel time, the tutor's weekly hours limit, a closure or outside opening hours.
Working with a lesson

Select a lesson to open its quick view: status, tutors, students, place, a link to the job, and How this was priced if you can see rates. From here:

  • Reschedule: enter the new date and times and a reason, then Move lesson. You can also drag a lesson in the day and week views. The people involved are told.
  • Mark completed: take the register (see attendance).
  • Mark missed: for a lesson that didn't happen and wasn't cancelled.
  • Cancel lesson: see cancellations.
  • Write report: open the lesson report.
Change many lessons at once

In the Agenda view, select Select lessons, tick the lessons and choose an Action: Mark complete, Cancel, Give to another tutor, Change location or Delete (planned lessons only). Select Apply to selected. Lessons that couldn't be changed stay selected so you can see them.

Times and time zones

The calendar shows times in your own time zone (set on your account). When a lesson's own time zone is different, the quick view shows its local time too. Repeating lessons keep their local clock time across daylight-saving changes.

Locked lessons

Once a lesson is on an issued invoice or in a paid pay run, it is locked: "This lesson is invoiced or paid, so it can't be changed here." Correct the money with a credit note or a pay adjustment instead.

Unconfirmed lessons

Unconfirmed lessons lists past lessons that nobody has marked as completed or cancelled. Unconfirmed lessons are not charged or paid, so clear this list before you run invoices or payroll. It needs scheduling.lesson.complete.

Tick lessons (or Select all) and select Mark completed, Cancel or Remind tutors. Lessons marked completed in bulk count every student as present. Your organisation can also complete lessons automatically after a number of hours.

Availability

Availability holds each tutor's regular hours and time off. Staff with scheduling.availability.manage_others choose a Tutor; others see their own.

  • Weekly availability: add windows with Add a time, choose whether each is in person, online or both, set Applies from and select Save availability.
  • Time off and extra availability: choose the type, the days and times, and select Add. Time off entered by staff is approved straight away, and you are told how many planned lessons clash and need cover.
  • Calendar feed: Create feed link gives an iCal link to subscribe to in any calendar app. A new link replaces the old one.

Requests

Requests collects things waiting for the office to approve. Each has Approve and Decline and an optional note.

  • Requests to move lessons: from families, tutors, or a lesson moved in a tutor's own calendar. Unanswered requests are declined automatically at the time shown. Needs scheduling.lesson.edit.
  • Online bookings to approve: when your booking settings need approval. Needs scheduling.lesson.create.
  • Time off to approve: approving flags the tutor's lessons in that time and looks for cover (see cover requests).

Online booking itself is set up by an admin in Online booking; see online booking in the admin guide.

Jobs

Jobs shows every job as a List or a Board. It needs jobs.job.view.

StatusMeaning

Tick Needs attention to find jobs that need action: seeking a tutor for over a week, an offer waiting, no upcoming lessons, a low prepaid balance, or overdue reports.

Create a job from the student page with Set up lessons: choose the Service, a Tutor (or Find a tutor later), an optional rate, the day, time and start date, and select Create job. The weekly series is created for you.

The job page shows its details (rate, billing method, schedule, hours cap), students, tutors and Economics (charge, pay and margin per lesson, if you can see rates).

  • Add a tutor: choose a tutor and select Assign. Tick Offer first (tutor accepts) to send an offer instead.
  • Replace: choose the new tutor and the date, select Preview to see how many lessons move and clash, then Replace tutor.
  • Change status: choose the status and a reason. Completing or cancelling a job can cancel its future lessons.
  • Find a tutor: opens matching for this job (see Matching).

If a job has an hours cap, you get a warning at 80% and scheduling past 100% is blocked.

Matching, offers and cover

To find a tutor for a job, open the job and select Find a tutor. TutorTrack ranks tutors by availability, distance, rating, experience, spare capacity, similar lessons taught, margin, offer acceptance and time since their last job. Open Score breakdown to see why a tutor ranks where they do. Restricted and unapproved tutors are hidden unless you tick Include restricted and unapproved tutors, and they can't be offered the job.

Students' needs. The Students' needs panel shows the Exam boards and Special educational needs recorded on the job's students. Tutors who teach one of the boards and have experience of those needs score higher (Exam board and SEN experience in the breakdown). If your organisation allows gender preferences, a student's Tutor gender preference limits the matches. Change any of them and select Search again to try a different search; the students' records don't change. SEN tags and gender preferences are only shown to staff who may see students' sensitive details.

  1. Select Shortlist on suitable tutors.
  2. Choose how to reach them:
    • Send offers: tick tutors in order, choose One after another or All at once and the hours each has to answer. The first to accept gets the job.
    • Post to the job board: eligible tutors can apply; pick one with Choose this tutor.
    • Share the shortlist with the family: the family gets a link showing tutor profiles (no surnames, contact details or scores) and picks one. Their choice starts an offer to that tutor.
  3. Follow progress under Offers. Where needed, Confirm tutor, Turn down or Withdraw offers.

The Matching page in the sidebar (matching.search) has:

  • Cover requests: lessons that need a stand-in, created when time off is approved or by tutors. Who's free lists suitable tutors; select Give them the cover. Tutors can also take cover themselves in the tutor app.
  • Analytics: time to match, offer acceptance and unmatched demand by subject and area over 90 days.
  • Weights: how much each ranking factor counts, including Exam board and SEN experience (which only count when a search asks for them), and whether families may state a Gender preferences (admins; see the admin guide).

You can also ask the AI command bar to find tutors.

Lessons and learning

Attendance and completing lessons

A lesson is charged and paid only when it is completed (or cancelled with a fee). Tutors normally complete their own lessons in the tutor app. The office can do it from the calendar quick view with Mark completed:

  1. Under Register, set each student's attendance: Present, Late, Absent (told us) or No-show. Mark all present sets everyone at once.
  2. If the lesson ran at different times, fill in Actual times (if different).
  3. Select Complete lesson.

Each outcome charges the client and pays the tutor a percentage set in your lesson policy. The tutor is paid in full if anyone attended. If a prepaid client doesn't have enough credit, completion is blocked; people with delivery.balance.override can tick Complete anyway (the client will owe this).

Cancellations and makeup credits

  1. Open the lesson and select Cancel lesson.
  2. Choose Who cancelled (Client, Student, Tutor or Us) and add a reason. TutorTrack shows what the policy means, for example "This is a late cancellation: client charged 100%, tutor paid 50%."
  3. If you are allowed, select Change the charge or pay to override the percentages, and say why. Overrides are recorded in the audit log.
  4. Tick Also cancel the rest of the series if the lessons are stopping (later lessons are cancelled free), and Tell the people involved.
  5. Select Cancel lesson.

A late-cancellation charge appears in billing as a separate charge. Depending on the policy, a free or tutor cancellation gives the family a makeup credit. Families use it when they book in the portal; makeup lessons are free. Credits expire after the number of days in the policy (60 by default).

The cancellation policy, free-cancellation limits and report templates are set in Lesson policies; see lesson policies in the admin guide.

Lesson reports

After each lesson the tutor writes a report from a template. Lesson reports (delivery.report.view) has the tabs Due, Overdue, To approve (if you approve reports) and Shared.

  • Open a report to read it. If your organisation requires approval, select Approve or Return to tutor with a note on What needs changing.
  • Share with client makes it visible in the portal. If approval isn't required, reports are shared automatically when submitted.
  • Use Add a comment to discuss it. Choose Staff and tutor only or Client too.

Reports have a deadline. Tutors get a reminder, then the report becomes overdue, and finally a task is created for the office. Your organisation can hold the tutor's pay for the lesson until the report is in. A report drafted with AI shows an AI-assisted badge.

Lesson feedback

If turned on, TutorTrack asks families to rate some lessons from 1 to 5 stars, with an optional comment. Lesson feedback (delivery.feedback.view) shows the Feedback summary (average stars, answer rate, low ratings and results By tutor) and every request with its status. Filter on Low ratings only to follow up unhappy families. Withdraw cancels a request that hasn't been answered.

Low ratings alert the office. Families can opt out of feedback requests. Admins turn requests on and set how often they go out in the Feedback requests section of this page.

Courses, classes and registers

Use Courses (courses.view) for group teaching. A course is the product (for example "GCSE Maths revision"), a class is a scheduled group with a number of places, and a term is the date range for enrolment and billing.

  • Terms: add academic years and terms, with breaks such as half term. Class sessions skip breaks.
  • Courses: New course sets the pricing (per term, per month, per session, whole course up front or in instalments), an optional yearly registration fee, a description, and Show on the website and portal.

Money

Completed lessons (and cancellations with a fee) become charges on the client's account. Invoices group charges for a period. Tutors' share becomes pay items, which are paid in pay runs.

Financial records are never edited after issue

Once an invoice is issued it can't be changed or deleted. To correct it, issue a credit note, void it or write it off. Pay items in a pay run work the same way: a change creates a linked correction item. Amounts are rounded half-up to the penny (or other smallest unit) on each line, and totals add up the rounded lines.

Calendar and video

Connect your own calendar so that TutorTrack knows when you are busy and puts your lessons in your calendar.

  • Select Connect Google, Connect Microsoft 365 (this covers Outlook) or Connect iCloud. For iCloud you need an app-specific password from appleid.apple.com.
  • Under Calendars that make you busy, choose which calendars block lesson times. Only the times are copied, never titles or details.
  • Under Calendar for lessons, choose where your lessons go, for example a separate "TutorTrack" calendar.
  • With Two-way sync, moving a lesson in your calendar asks the office to reschedule it. Deleting it there never cancels it.

Under My video provider for online lessons, choose the organisation default, the built-in room, Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Google Meet. Connect Zoom links your own Zoom account.

Jobs, the job board and cover

  • Job offers: the office offers you new students. Each offer shows the lessons, students, place, times, start date and pay rate, and when you must answer by. Select Accept or Decline. Offers can go to several tutors at once, so answer quickly.
  • Job board: open jobs the office has posted. Add an optional message and select Apply. The status changes to You got the job or Another tutor was chosen.
  • Cover: lessons another tutor can't teach. Select I'll cover it to take them. To ask for cover yourself, add time off or contact the office.

Homework and resources

Homework shows work Handed in, waiting for review. Open an item, add a Grade and Feedback, then select Mark complete or Return for resubmission.

To set homework, use Set homework: enter a title, choose one student, the due date and instructions. Students answer in writing. (The office can set other kinds, such as file uploads and quizzes.) Homework you add in a lesson report is also set for you automatically.

Resources lists the organisation library and your own links. To share one, choose the student under Share … with and select Share. To add a link, enter a title and web address under Add a resource.

Earnings and expenses

Earnings (if your organisation shows it) has:

  • Coming up, Paid this year and Next pay.
  • On hold: pay that can't go out yet, with the reason, for example "Write the lesson report to release this." or "Paid once the family pays the invoice."
  • Payouts and statements: your self-billing invoices or remittance advice as PDFs.
  • Earnings summary: choose a year and select Download PDF for your records (UK organisations use the tax year).
  • Tutors paid in US dollars enter their taxpayer id for the 1099-NEC form here.

To claim an expense, open Expenses, choose the Category, date and what it was for. For mileage, enter the distance or select Suggest from my lessons to work it out from that day's lessons. Add a Receipt photo and select Send claim. The office approves or rejects it; any comment shows in the list. Approved claims are paid in your next pay run.

Check invoices

Some organisations ask tutors to check their lessons before invoices go to families. When there is something to check, Check invoices lists your lessons for the period (date, lesson, student, minutes; never amounts).

  • If they are right, select These are right.
  • If not, select Something's wrong, say what is wrong and select Send. The office holds those invoices until it is sorted out.

Answer by the date shown; after that the invoices go out as they are.

Subject tests

Subject tests lists short online tests for the subjects you teach, with the number of questions and the pass mark.

  1. Select Start …, answer each question and select Send answers.
  2. Your score shows straight away. If you pass, the subject is marked as assessed and the office approves it.
  3. If you don't pass, you can try again from the date shown (a week later by default).

Getting started and checks

New tutors see a checklist in Getting started. Items marked * are required before you can teach. Some you tick with Mark done; agreements you read and sign with Sign … (see signing documents); others complete themselves when you do them elsewhere in the app. When everything is done you see "You're all set."

Checks lists the documents and checks you must hold, such as a DBS check, right to work, ID and qualifications. Each shows Missing, Being checked, Verified, Rejected (with the reason) or Expired.

  1. Find the requirement, and enter the reference number, issue date and expiry date.
  2. Choose the Document (a photo or PDF) and select Send.
  3. The office checks it and marks it verified, or rejects it with a reason.

If the office started an online background check, Online background checks has a Complete your check link to the provider.

Expired checks

You get reminders 60, 30 and 7 days before a document expires. If a required check expires, you see "You can't take new lessons until your checks are up to date." and pay can be held until it is fixed.

Profile and pay details

  • Edit your Headline, Phone and About you (shown to families), then Save.
  • Public profile (if your organisation has a tutor directory on its website): write your public bio, add a photo, choose what else to show and select Save and submit. The office approves changes before they appear. Don't include contact details or links.
  • Pay details: enter the name on the account, sort code and account number and select Save bank details. Self-employed tutors also agree to self-billing here, so that TutorTrack can issue invoices on their behalf.

Notifications

Turn on push notifications to get lesson changes, job offers, cover requests, report reminders, pay updates and messages on your phone.

  1. On an iPhone, add the app to your Home Screen first (see installing the app).
  2. Open Notifications and select Turn on notifications. Allow notifications when the browser asks.
  3. Select Send a test notification to check it works.

Devices with notifications on lists your devices; select Remove for one you no longer use. Under What you hear about, and how, choose the channels (email, text, in the app, push, WhatsApp) for each kind of message. Important messages still come by email if you turn everything off. You can also choose digest emails such as a Daily agenda.

Referrals and messages

Referrals ("Refer and earn") has a Link for families and a Link for tutors. Copy a link and share it. You earn a bonus, added to your pay, when a tutor you refer starts teaching or a family you refer pays.

Messages works like the family messages (see Messages). You can write to the office or to the families you teach. Messages are checked by safeguarding filters, and the office can read all conversations.

Clients and students: the client portal

The client portal is where parents, guardians, adult learners and students see their lessons, reports, homework and invoices. It is at /portal/ on your tutoring organisation's web address, and carries the organisation's logo and colours.

Signing in to the portal

You get access when the office invites you. There are two ways to sign in:

  • Parents, guardians and students with an email address: enter your Email and select Email me a sign-in link. Open the link in the email. There is no password to remember.
  • Children under 13 with a login made by a parent: under Child sign-in, enter the Username and PIN or password and select Sign in with username. After several wrong attempts, sign-in slows down for a while.

On a phone, the tabs along the bottom take you to Home, Lessons, Payments and Messages (for students, the last two tabs are the first two of Messages, Learning and Reports that are turned on). More opens everything else, grouped under Lessons and learning and Payments and your family. On a larger screen the same items are listed down the left. To sign out, select your initials at the top right, then Sign out.

Some menu items only appear when your tutoring organisation turns them on, so your menu may be shorter than the one described here. When an item is turned off, the next one moves up into the tabs. (Office staff: see portal configuration in the admin guide.)

Home and switching households

Home shows any amount you owe (with Pay now), then your Next lesson: the day, the time, the tutor and place, and its buttons (Join the lesson for online lessons, Add to calendar, and, if your organisation allows them, Cancel lesson, Ask to move and Can't make it). Below are the lessons This week (with All lessons), the Latest reports and News from your tutoring organisation.

If you belong to more than one household (for example, separated parents with children in two accounts), a household selector appears in the bar at the top. Choose one household or All households.

The family portal needs an internet connection. When you are offline it says so; reconnect to see the latest lessons, reports and invoices.

Lessons

Lessons lists lessons from the past week to six weeks ahead. If you have several children, use the Student filter. Each lesson shows the time, tutor, place or Online, and any notes for you.

  • Join the lesson: for online lessons. It is greyed out until the room opens, shortly before the start.
  • Open the classroom: opens the online classroom page (see the online classroom).
  • Add to calendar: downloads the lesson for your own calendar.
  • Cancel lesson: shows what the cancellation policy means for this lesson first, for example whether there is a late-cancellation fee. Add a reason if you like and select Confirm cancellation, or Keep the lesson. For lessons booked online there can be a cut-off, after which you see "To cancel this lesson now, please contact us."
  • Ask to move: suggest a New time and select Send request. The lesson stays where it is until the tutor or office agrees.
  • Can't make it: tell the tutor a student can't come. In a one-to-one lesson the lesson is cancelled under the cancellation policy; in a group lesson the student is marked absent.

Book a lesson

If your organisation offers online booking, Book lets you choose a time yourself.

  1. Choose the Lesson, the Tutor (or Any tutor), the student and the week under Show times from. Enter a Promo code if you have one.
  2. If you have a makeup credit from a cancelled lesson, choose it under Use a makeup credit. A makeup lesson booked with a credit is free.
  3. Select a free time. TutorTrack holds it for you for a few minutes and shows the price.
  4. Select Book. Depending on your organisation, the lesson is booked straight away, you are asked to pay to confirm, or the office confirms it soon.

When a tutor asks to move one of your lessons, the request appears at the top of this page under Requests to move a lesson. Select Accept or Decline.

Classes

  • Classes you can join lists open group classes with days, term, place, price and places left. Choose Who is joining, accept the terms if asked and select Enrol …. If the class is full, you can Join the waitlist. If payment is needed you go to the pay page; the place is held until the date shown.
  • Your classes shows enrolments and their status. Give up this place releases a held place.
  • Waitlist shows your position. If a place comes up, you get an email with a link to accept it.
  • Next term asks whether you want to keep your place next term. Select Keep the place or Not continuing by the date shown.

Reports

Reports shows the lesson reports your tutors have shared with you, with any attachments to download. To reply, use Reply to the tutor and Send reply; the tutor is told.

Learning: homework, resources and progress

Learning has these tabs: Homework, Resources, Goals, Results, Progress reports and Borrowed items.

  1. In Homework, open a piece of homework. It shows the instructions, roughly how long it takes and any resources.
  2. Answer in the way the tutor asked: write your answer, add a link, upload files, take the quiz, or just select Mark as done.
  3. Select Hand in. Quizzes are marked straight away and show your score.
  4. When the tutor has looked at it, the status changes to Marked (with feedback and a grade) or Returned for changes.

Goals shows targets and milestones, Results shows a chart of scores over time (with Show as a table), Progress reports has termly or monthly reports to Download PDF, and Borrowed items lists books or equipment on loan with their due-back dates.

Payments

Payments shows, for each account, what is Owed, Overdue and in Credit, with Download a statement (PDF).

  • Pay an invoice: under Invoices, select Pay …. A secure pay page opens. Depending on your organisation you can pay by card, direct debit or PayPal. Each invoice also has a PDF.
  • Pay automatically: under Payment methods, select Add a card or direct debit and agree to automatic payments. Invoices are then paid on their due date. Stop paying automatically turns this off. You can also Make default or Remove a method.
  • Packages: see the hours left on prepaid packages and their expiry date. To buy one, choose the account and select Buy; you then pay the package invoice.
  • Top-up requests: requests from your organisation to add credit before lessons. Select Pay.
  • Credit notes: corrections to earlier invoices. Issued invoices are never changed; a credit note reduces what you owe or adds credit.

Details

  • Your details: your name and phone numbers, and What you'd like to receive (lesson reminders, invoices, lesson reports, news and offers). To change your email, ask the office.
  • For each child: preferred name, school, year group and Learning needs, medical or support information. Only staff and the child's tutors see this, and the office is told when it changes.
  • Digest emails: choose a Weekly schedule email and others your organisation offers, and when to get them.

Family and logins

Invite another parent or guardian. Under Parents and guardians, use Invite another guardian. Enter their name, email and relationship, confirm that they have parental responsibility or your permission, and select Send invitation. They have 7 days to accept.

Give a child under 13 their own login. Under Logins for children under 13:

  1. Find the child and enter a Username (lower-case letters, numbers, dots or dashes) and a PIN or password of at least 6 characters.
  2. Tick the consent box and select Create login. Your consent is recorded.
  3. Tell your child their username and PIN. They sign in with Child sign-in.

Use Change PIN if the PIN gets out; your child is signed out everywhere. Remove login removes it. Children aged 13 or over get their own email login: ask the office to invite them.

Documents and signing

Documents lists documents the office has shared, such as terms or consent forms. Each shows No signature needed, Waiting for a signature or Signed on ….

  1. Select Download … and read the document.
  2. Select Sign …, type Your full name and tick that you have read and agree to it.
  3. Select Sign. Your name, the time, your IP address and browser are recorded, and a signed copy is kept on your record.

Refer a friend

If your organisation runs a referral programme, Refer a friend explains the reward and gives Your referral link (Copy link) and a code. Families you referred shows each referral's status and reward. Rewards are added as account credit.

Messages

Select New message, choose who it is To (The office, or a tutor if your organisation allows it), write a Subject and Your message, attach files if needed and select Send. Replies appear under Conversations; the list refreshes every 30 seconds.

To keep children safe, messages are checked by safeguarding filters and staff can read all conversations. A student under 18 can only start a conversation that includes a parent, unless the organisation has changed this.

The online classroom and recordings

Open the classroom on an online lesson opens a page with the lesson details, any safeguarding message from your organisation and the lesson resources.

  • The built-in room (and Lessonspace) opens inside the page; allow the camera and microphone. Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Google Meet lessons open in their own app with Join the lesson.
  • If the lesson is recorded, you are told. If your consent is needed, select I agree to recording.
  • Before the start you see when the room opens; the page refreshes by itself.
  • Resources for this lesson lists what was shared with the lesson, the job or your child. Select Open next to one to open it in a new tab. While the room is open the list refreshes every few seconds, so something shared during the lesson appears straight away.
  • Tutors can put one of their resources into the room: choose it under Resource, add a Message (optional) and select Share into the room. Families also get a notice that links to the classroom.

Recordings lists lesson recordings shared with you. Select Watch to play one.

Your data

Under Make a request, choose A copy of my data, My data in a machine-readable file or Erase my data, and whether it is about you or a child. Select Send request. You get a reference and the date by which the organisation will answer (within a month). Copies arrive as a download link by email. Your requests shows progress.

Erasure can't be undone

The organisation keeps only what the law requires, such as invoices.

To change what you agreed to, such as marketing messages, use What you'd like to receive in Details or contact the office.

Affiliates: the partner portal

Affiliates are partners (people or organisations) who refer new families and earn commission. The office adds you and sends a portal invitation. Sign in at /portal/ with the emailed sign-in link; you go straight to the partner portal. If you see "This login isn't linked to a partner account.", contact the office.

On a phone, the four pages are tabs along the bottom; on a larger screen they are listed down the left. To sign out, select your initials at the top right, then Sign out.

Overview

Overview shows Visits to your links, families Referred and Paying clients, what you have earned and how much is not yet paid. Under Your tracking links, select Copy link and share the link. Families who arrive through it are credited to you.

Referred clients

Referred clients lists the families you referred (names are shortened for privacy), when they joined, their first payment and the commission earned so far.

Statements

The office issues statements, usually monthly. Each shows the period, total and whether it is Issued or Paid, with its lines: Commission, Bounty, Refund and Adjustment. If a referred family is refunded soon after paying, the commission on that payment can be taken back.

Payout details

Enter the Name on the account, Sort code and Account number and select Save payout details. After saving, only the last digits are shown. Statements are paid by bank transfer.

Common tasks

Step-by-step recipes for jobs the office and tutors do every week.

Add a family and book weekly lessons

  1. Search first (Ctrl+K) to make sure the family isn't already there.
  2. Go to Clients and select Add a family.
  3. Enter the parent or guardian's name, email and phone, each student's first name, year group and subject, and the billing address. Select Create family.
  4. On the family page, select Invite to portal next to the parent so they can see lessons and pay invoices.
  5. Open the student and select Set up lessons.
  6. Choose the Service and the tutor, or Find a tutor later. Leave the rate empty to use the service rate.
  7. Enter the day, time and start date and select Create job. The weekly lessons appear in the calendar.
  8. If you chose to find a tutor later, follow find a tutor for a new student.

Find a tutor for a new student

  1. Open the job (from Jobs, tick Needs attention to find jobs seeking a tutor) and select Find a tutor.
  2. Look at the top matches and their Score breakdown and availability. Shortlist two or three.
  3. Select Send offers, tick the tutors in your order of preference, choose One after another and the hours each has to answer, and select Send offers.
  4. Tutors answer in the tutor app. The first to accept is assigned and the job becomes active. If the family should choose, use Share the shortlist with the family instead.

Take the register and write the report (tutors)

  1. In the tutor app, open Today and select Open and complete on the lesson.
  2. Under Register, set each student's attendance.
  3. Select Complete and write report.
  4. Fill in the report. Use Dictate to speak into text fields, or Draft with AI from rough notes if available.
  5. Select Submit report. No signal? It is saved on the phone and sent when you are back online.

Cancel a lesson when a family calls

  1. Find the lesson in the Calendar and select it.
  2. Select Cancel lesson and choose Who cancelled: Client.
  3. Read the policy message: it tells you whether this is a late cancellation and what is charged and paid.
  4. Tick Tell the people involved and select Cancel lesson.
  5. If the policy gives a makeup credit, the family can use it when they book in the portal.

Run monthly invoices and chase payment

  1. Clear Unconfirmed lessons: mark every past lesson completed or cancelled, so that it is charged correctly.
  2. Go to Billing › Invoice runs and select Run invoicing. Choose last month and Delivered lessons and charges. Select Preview, then start the run.
  3. When the run is In review, check the drafts and any tutor problems.
  4. Select Issue now. Families are emailed and those with auto-pay are charged on the due date.
  5. Later, check Billing › Invoices with Show set to overdue, or the Ageing tab. Reminders go out automatically.
  6. For a family that needs a nudge, open the invoice and select Email to client, or message them from the Inbox. If they have a saved card, Collect now.

Record a bank transfer or cash payment

  1. Open the client's page and, in the billing section, select Record payment.
  2. Choose How they paid (for example Bank transfer), the date received and the bank reference.
  3. Leave the default to pay the oldest invoices first, or choose which invoices it pays.
  4. Save. Anything left over becomes credit for future invoices.

For several payments at once, use Billing › Record payments, or import your bank statement on the Bank tab and confirm the suggested matches.

Correct an invoice that has been issued

  1. Open the invoice. Issued invoices can't be edited.
  2. If the whole invoice is wrong and nothing has been paid or credited, select Void and give a reason. The charges go back to Not yet invoiced and the lessons are unlocked, so you can fix them and invoice again.
  3. Otherwise select Credit note, enter the amount to credit on each wrong line, choose whether to reduce this invoice or keep it as client credit, and select Issue credit note.
  4. If you need to charge more, use Add a charge on the client's page; it goes on the next invoice.

Run payroll

  1. Approve or reject waiting claims in Payroll › Expense claims.
  2. Check Payroll › Pay items for items On hold, and chase missing reports or compliance documents.
  3. Go to Payroll, select New pay run and choose the period.
  4. Open the run, read the warnings and correct any item with Edit.
  5. Select Approve (a second approver may be needed).
  6. Select Make bank file, upload it to your bank, then Mark all paid. Tutors see their statements in the tutor app.

Turn an enquiry into a client

  1. Open the enquiry from Enquiries. Call or email the family and move the card to Contacted.
  2. Under Next steps, choose the Service, enter the Trial lesson starts time and select Book free trial.
  3. After the trial, set the Trial outcome to Continuing and select Save outcome.
  4. Tick Invite the family to the portal and select Convert to client.
  5. A client, the students and a job seeking a tutor are created. Assign the trial tutor on the job, or find a tutor.

Take on a new tutor

  1. In Recruitment, open the application, invite the applicant to interview and ask for references.
  2. Select Approve and onboard. The tutor gets an invitation and a Getting started checklist.
  3. The tutor signs agreements, adds bank details and uploads documents in Checks. Start an online background check from their page if you use one.
  4. Verify each document from the tutor's compliance card. When the required items are done, the tutor becomes active.
  5. Approve the subjects they teach on their page. They now appear in matching.

Glossary

Affiliate
A partner who refers new families and earns commission on what they pay.
Branch
Part of an organisation, such as a location or brand. It can have its own currency, time zone and staff.
Charge
Something a client owes: a lesson, a cancellation fee, a package, a registration or late fee. Charges are grouped onto invoices.
Class, course and term
Group teaching: a course is the product, a class is a scheduled group with places, a term is the date range for enrolment and billing.
Client
The billing account: a family, an adult learner or an organisation such as a school.
Contact
A person attached to a client, such as a parent. One contact is the bill payer.
Cover request
A request for another tutor to teach lessons a tutor can't, for example during time off.
Credit
Money a client has paid in advance or overpaid, used to pay future invoices.
Credit note
A document that reduces an issued invoice or adds client credit. The only way to correct an issued invoice apart from voiding it.
Enquiry
A request from a prospective family, tracked through the sales pipeline until it is won or lost.
Invoice run
Invoicing many clients at once for a period.
Job
An ongoing arrangement between a client, students and tutors for a service, with agreed rates and a schedule. Lessons belong to it.
Lesson report
The tutor's notes after a lesson. It can be shared with the family.
Locked lesson
A lesson on an issued invoice or a paid pay run. It can't be changed; corrections are made with credit notes or pay adjustments.
Makeup credit
A free lesson given for some cancellations, which the family uses when booking.
Package
A prepaid bundle of hours that lessons use up, often with an expiry date.
Payment request
A request for prepayment that tops up a client's credit. It is not an invoice.
Pay item
A line of tutor earnings: lesson pay, an expense, a bonus or a deduction.
Pay run
A batch of pay items for a period that is approved and paid together.
Restricted tutor
A tutor whose required checks have expired. They can't be given new lessons until it is fixed.
Self-billing
An arrangement where the organisation issues invoices on behalf of a self-employed tutor.
Series
A repeating set of lessons, such as every Tuesday at 4pm.
Service
A kind of tuition in the catalogue, such as "GCSE Maths, online, one-to-one", with default length and rates.
Student
A learner. Each student belongs to one client.
