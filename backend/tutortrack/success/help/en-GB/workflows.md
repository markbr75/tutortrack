**Workflows** decides when TutorTrack does things for you: when invoices go out, when
payment and lesson reminders are sent, how enquiries are followed up and when expiry
reminders fire. (It used to be called Automations.)

To change one of TutorTrack's own workflows:

1. Go to **Workflows** and open it from the **Built-in** tab, for example **Invoicing cycle**.
2. Select **Edit** on a card. The **Start** card says what starts it; each card below says
   when a step happens and what it does, for example "3 days before “Due”, at 09:00".
3. Choose the new timing and select **Save**. Runs already under way finish on the old steps.

To check a workflow before you rely on it, choose a family, lesson, invoice or enquiry under
**Preview for a record** and select **Preview**: you see the real dates and what would be
sent. Nothing is sent. **Coming up** lists the next dates a scheduled workflow runs.

The **Runs** tab shows every run and where it is, for example "Waiting until 12 Nov, 09:00".
You can **Cancel** a run under way, or select **Retry from the failed step** on one that
failed. **Reset to default** puts a built-in back as TutorTrack ships it.

To make your own, select **New workflow** or use one of the **Templates**. An older
automation with a split inside a split still runs, but opens read-only: contact support from
**Help** to change it.
