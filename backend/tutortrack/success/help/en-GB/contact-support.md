---
title: Contact TutorTrack support
summary: Chat with our support team from the help panel.
routes: /
tags: support, chat, help, contact
---
Open the help panel and select **Chat with support**. Write your question and send it. We
reply in the same conversation, and the help button shows a dot when there is a reply.

Do not send passwords, card numbers or safeguarding details in the chat.

To control when TutorTrack support can look at your account, the owner goes to
**Settings → TutorTrack support access**.
