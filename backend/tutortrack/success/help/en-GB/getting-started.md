The checklist on **Home** shows the first steps. Each step is ticked when you do it in the
app, so you always see your progress.

1. **Set up a service.** A service is something you sell, for example "GCSE Maths, one to
   one, 60 minutes". Go to **Catalogue** and select **New service**.
2. **Add a family.** On **Home**, select **Add a family**. You add the family's contacts
   and their students together.
3. **Schedule a lesson.** Open the **Calendar** and select **New lesson**, or open the
   student and select **Set up lessons**.
4. **Connect Stripe.** Go to **Settings → Payments setup** so that families can pay by card.
5. **Send your first invoice.** Go to **Billing**, or open the family and select
   **Invoice now**.

If you teach your own lessons, tick **I teach lessons myself** in the setup wizard, or
select **Add yourself as a tutor** on the tutors page. You can hide the checklist when you
no longer need it. To see it again, use the help panel.
