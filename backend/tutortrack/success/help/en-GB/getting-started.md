The checklist on your dashboard shows the first steps. Each step is ticked when you do it
in the app, so you always see your progress.

1. **Set up a service.** A service is something you sell, for example "GCSE Maths, one to
   one, 60 minutes". Go to **Catalogue** and add one.
2. **Add a student.** Go to **Students**, then add the student and their family.
3. **Schedule a lesson.** Open the **Calendar** and select a time.
4. **Connect Stripe.** Go to **Settings → Payments** so that families can pay by card.
5. **Send your first invoice.** Go to **Billing**, make an invoice and send it.

You can hide the checklist when you no longer need it. To see it again, use the help
panel.
