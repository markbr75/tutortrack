In TutorTrack a **client** is the account that pays: usually a family, sometimes a school
or an adult learner. A **student** belongs to one client.

To add a family:

1. On **Home** or **Clients**, select **Add a family**.
2. Enter the contact who pays, and any other contacts who get invoices and reminders.
3. Add the family's students.

To invite a parent to the portal, open the family and select **Invite to portal** next to
the contact. In the portal they see lessons, invoices and lesson reports.

To change a name, phone number or address later, see "Change a person's details".
