In TutorTrack a **client** is the account that pays: usually a family, sometimes a school
or an adult learner. A **student** belongs to one client.

To add a student:

1. Go to **Students** and select **Add student**.
2. Choose the family, or make a new one.
3. Add the contacts who get invoices and reminders.

You can invite families to the portal. In the portal they see lessons, invoices and
lesson reports.
