Completed lessons become charges on the family's account. An invoice groups the charges
for a period.

1. Go to **Billing**.
2. Select **New invoice**, or run an invoice run for many families at the same time.
3. Check the invoice, then select **Issue and send**.

You cannot change an issued invoice. To correct it, issue a credit note.
