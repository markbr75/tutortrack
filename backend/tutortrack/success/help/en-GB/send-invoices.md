Completed lessons become charges on the family's account. An invoice groups the charges
for a period.

To invoice every family at once:

1. Go to **Billing → Invoice runs** and select **Run invoicing**.
2. Choose the period, then select **Preview** and start the run.
3. Check the draft invoices, then select **Issue now**. Families get their invoices by email.

To invoice one family, open the family and select **Invoice now**. On a draft invoice,
select **Issue invoice** when it is correct.

You can't change an issued invoice. To correct it, open it and issue a **Credit note**.
