title: Working on your own
summary: How TutorTrack works for a sole trader who teaches every lesson.

If you set **Type of business** to **Sole trader**, TutorTrack keeps the team tools out of
your way:

- **You are the only tutor.** The menu has **My tutor profile** instead of a list of
  tutors. It opens your own subjects, rates and details.
- **A shorter menu.** Jobs, Matching, Payroll, Recruitment and Compliance are for running a
  team of tutors, so they are hidden. Home shows what is **Not yet invoiced** instead of the
  next pay run.
- **No second tutor.** You can't add or invite another tutor.

When you take on tutors, go to **Settings → Organisation** and change **Type of business**.
The team menus come back and you can add tutors. To change back to sole trader, make the
other tutors inactive or archive them first.
