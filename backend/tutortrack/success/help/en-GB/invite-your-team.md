---
title: Invite your team and set roles
summary: Give staff and tutors their own logins with the right permissions.
routes: /team, /team/roles
tags: team, roles, permissions, invite, staff
---
1. Go to **Team**.
2. Under **Invite someone**, enter the email address and choose a role, for example
   Coordinator or Finance.
3. Select **Send invitation**. The person gets an email with a link to join. The link
   works for 7 days.

Roles control what each person can see and do. To change someone's role, use the list next
to their name. For a different mix of permissions, make a custom role on **Team → Roles**.

A sole trader can't invite tutors, because they teach every lesson themselves.
