---
title: Invite your team and set roles
summary: Give staff and tutors their own logins with the right permissions.
routes: /team, /team/roles
tags: team, roles, permissions, invite, staff
---
1. Go to **Team** and select **Invite**.
2. Enter the email address and choose a role, for example Coordinator or Finance.
3. The person gets an email with a link to join.

Roles control what each person can see and do. You can copy a built-in role and change it.
You can ask staff to use two-factor authentication.
