Go to **Billing → Invoice templates** and select **New template**. The preview on the right
shows a sample invoice as you change the settings.

1. Give the template a name, for example "Music lessons".
2. Under **Look**, choose a style (Classic, Modern or Compact), an accent colour and a logo.
3. Under **Wording**, set the title (for example "Tax invoice"), a trading name, a message
   and how to pay.
4. Under **Columns**, choose what shows on each line.
5. Select **Create template**. To check the real file, select **Download sample PDF**.

The default template is used for every family. To use another one for a family, open the
family and edit **Billing details**. For a job, edit **Invoices** on the job page; this is
used when the family's invoices are one per job. An invoice keeps the look it was sent with.
