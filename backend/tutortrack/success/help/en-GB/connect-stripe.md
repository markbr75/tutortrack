TutorTrack uses Stripe Connect. The money goes to your own Stripe account, and Stripe pays
it out to your bank.

1. Go to **Settings → Payments setup**.
2. Select **Connect Stripe**, then follow the steps on Stripe.
3. When you come back, select **Check status**. The card shows **Ready to take payments**
   when Stripe has everything it needs.

You can also connect GoCardless for Direct Debit and PayPal.
