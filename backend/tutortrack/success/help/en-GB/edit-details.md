---
title: Change a person's details
summary: Correct a name, phone number, address or school on a family, student or tutor.
routes: /clients, /students, /tutors
tags: edit, details, name, address, phone, contact, profile, rename
---
Open the family, student or tutor. The **Details** panel (on a family, **Billing details**)
has **Edit**.

1. Select **Edit**. The fields open in place.
2. Change what you need.
3. Select **Save**, or **Cancel** to keep the details as they were.

Only the fields you change are saved. If a field has a problem, the message is next to it.
On a family, each contact has its own **Edit** for their name, email and phone numbers.

A tutor who has joined signs in with their email, so their email can't be changed here.
If you teach, changing your own name in **Account** also changes it on your tutor record.
