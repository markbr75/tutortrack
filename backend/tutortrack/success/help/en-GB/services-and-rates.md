A **service** is a type of tuition. It has a subject and level (optional), a delivery
mode (in person, online or both), a format (one to one or a group), a default length, a
charge rate and a pay rate.

To add a service:

1. Go to **Catalogue**.
2. Select **Add service**.
3. Give the service a name and the default rates.

You can change rates for one job, student or tutor later. The charge rate is what the
family pays. The pay rate is what the tutor earns.
