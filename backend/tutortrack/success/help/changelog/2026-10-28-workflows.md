---
title: Workflows
summary: Decide when invoices go out and when reminders are sent, and see every run.
date: 2026-10-28
---
- **Automations is now Workflows.** Your automations moved across as they were, and the
  recipes are now **Templates**. Old links still work.
- **Built-in workflows.** Invoicing, payment chasing, statements, lesson reminders, report
  chasing, feedback requests, enquiry follow-ups, review requests and expiry reminders are
  now workflows you can open and change: each step reads as a sentence, such as "3 days
  before the due date, at 09:00 · Send a payment reminder". Your current settings carried
  over, so nothing changes until you change it. **Reset to default** undoes your changes.
- **Preview and Coming up.** Choose a real family, lesson, invoice or enquiry to see the
  dates each step would happen and what it would send, without sending anything. A
  scheduled workflow lists its next five dates.
- **Runs.** See where each run is ("Waiting until 12 Nov, 09:00"), cancel one under way or
  retry one that failed. Integrators can subscribe to the new `workflow.run_failed` webhook.
