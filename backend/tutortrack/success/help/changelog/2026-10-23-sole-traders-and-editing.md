---
title: Sole traders, and editing details
summary: A simpler TutorTrack for one tutor, and Edit on every person's details.
date: 2026-10-23
---
- **Working on your own.** If your type of business is **Sole trader**, the menu shows
  **My tutor profile** instead of a list of tutors, and Jobs is hidden with the other team
  tools. You are the only tutor, so TutorTrack doesn't let you add another one.
- **Edit details.** Families, contacts, students and tutors now have **Edit** on their
  details, so you can correct a name, phone number or address in place.
- **The full guides.** The help panel now links to the user guide and the admin guide,
  starting at the section about the page you are on.
