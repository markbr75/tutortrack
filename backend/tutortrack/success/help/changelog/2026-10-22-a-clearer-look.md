---
title: A clearer look
summary: Every screen now follows one calm, consistent design.
date: 2026-10-22
---
Every screen in TutorTrack now uses the same layout: the page title and its actions at the
top, the record's work in the wide column and its details beside it. Lists share one filter
bar, and the family portal and tutor app have tabs along the bottom on a phone, with a small
number when something is waiting for you.
